# ⚡ FitPulse - AI-Powered Personal Health & Workout Monitor

FitPulse is a comprehensive, full-stack fitness and health tracking application built with the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React/Vanilla HTML/JS, Node.js). It leverages the **Google Gemini AI API** to provide users with personalized, AI-generated diet and workout routines based on their unique body metrics and goals.

## 🚀 Features

*   **Dashboard Overview:** A centralized hub to track your daily health snapshots, including active goals, total workouts, and total calories burned.
*   **AI Diet Recommendations:** Uses Google Gemini AI to analyze your age, weight, height, and goal to generate a personalized 1-day meal plan.
*   **AI Workout Generator:** Generates a custom workout routine tailored to your fitness level and available equipment.
*   **Workout Logger:** Full CRUD operations to track your fitness sessions, duration, and intensity.
*   **Goal Tracking:** Set, edit, and track long-term and short-term fitness goals.
*   **Interactive Analytics:** Dynamic, real-time charts (using `Chart.js`) showcasing weekly activity and workout breakdowns.
*   **BMI Calculator:** Calculate your Body Mass Index and instantly see which health category you fall into.
*   **Water Tracker:** An interactive, gamified water intake logger.
